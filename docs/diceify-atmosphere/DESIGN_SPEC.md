# Diceify continuous-atmosphere redesign

## Product and user job

Diceify is a high-density Random Dice 2 research tool. The home page first establishes that the data is verified, then moves players directly into the Dice Tree or another utility. It must feel like a game companion, not a promotional template.

## Visual direction

- Foundation: Root Night `#071018`, App Ink `#09131C`, Surface `#0B1721`, Raised `#101D26`.
- Atmosphere: Deep Navy `#031540`, Cobalt `#073B78`, Cyan `#27D5FF`.
- Type: Pretendard/SUIT system stack, compact utility labels, heavy but restrained display type.
- Signature element: the Random Dice 2 branching topology continues below the hero into a 220 to 300 pixel atmospheric transition.
- Surfaces below the hero stay solid. Gradients belong to the large-scale atmosphere, not buttons or utility cards.

## Structure

1. A translucent, blue-charcoal header occupies the same visual field as the hero and gains opacity after scrolling.
2. The preserved centered headline and white CTA sit above a low-contrast central veil.
3. The expanded topology art is allowed to escape the hero and resolve naturally through the transition.
4. The data freshness strip starts the dense application area, followed by utility and research modules.

## Motion and responsive behavior

Four large radial light fields move asynchronously over 24 to 34 seconds using transform and opacity only. The topology breathes by seven pixels over 27 seconds. Reduced-motion mode disables both. Mobile uses a dedicated wider art crop, shorter transition, and reduced edge-light footprint.

## Anti-template constraints

No purple gradients, rainbow effects, cursor-following glows, gradient buttons, decorative cards inside the hero, or speculative game metrics. The hero remains a single uninterrupted hierarchy: evidence label, headline, explanation, CTA, topology.
